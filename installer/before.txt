OptiGame+ finds the games on this PC, recommends graphics settings for each, and clears resources while you play.

What to expect

- Every change is previewed first. Nothing is written until you click Apply.
- Each Apply makes a backup of the file it changes, and you can undo it key by key.
- Apply refuses while the game is running.
- Boost closes background apps only when you click it. Auto-boost never closes anything on its own.
- Your settings, backups and history live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\GameOpt. Installing, updating or uninstalling does not touch them.
- It installs for your user only, so it does not need administrator rights.

If you used GameClear before, OptiGame+ imports its list and settings the first time it starts.
